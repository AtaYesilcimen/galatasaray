Welcome to, Galatasaray.

The greatest turkish football (soccer) club; and by some, the greatest ever.

History of the club

The club was founded in 1905, by students attending the Galatasaray highschool which still stands today. The highschool is Turkeys oldest highshool, being founded in 1481.

Founded specifically by Ali Sami Yen and his friends for the purpose of playing like the english at the time and dominating non turkish clubs.

Quickly became Turkeys most successful club, absolutely wrecking other Turkish teams. However, not so much success against foreign teams...yet.

Galatasaray went through numerous design choices, until finally landing on the popular yellow and red we all know today.

Competition

Galatasaray has two main rivals

  1. Fenerbahce
    • Their derby is known as the Intercontinental Derby
    • Feircest rivalry in all of Turkish football history
    • In 405 matches played throughout their history
      • Galatasaray has won 130 times
      • Drew 125 times
      • Lost 150 times
      • Though more losses than wins, in the last 20 years, Galatasaray has been extremely dominate
  2. Besiktas (You might know this one, for they had a really great canadian player in the past)
    • Though not as big of a rivalry as the other, this derby is also extremely anticipated and led with passion
    • In 350 matches played throughout their history
      • Galatasaray has won 128 times
      • Drew 115 times
      • Lost 116
      • Galatasaray has a very slight edge on besiktas

Domestic Leagues

Galatasaray participates in 3 major leagues in Turkey every year.

  1. The Super league (Süper Lig)
    • This is the highest level of football in Turkey
    • Galatasaray is first place on the table right now at the time this project was made.
    • Galatasaray has won the league three years in a row at the time this project was made
    • Galatasaray has 25 league titles, the most in Turkish Football history.
    • Galatasaray on their logo has 5 stars, gaining a star every 5 titles a club wins
  2. Turkish cup (Turkiye Kupasi)
    • A small tournament styled competion hosing the top 8 teams in the super league
    • The 3 highest placed, and the first place in the super league, go ahead into another competition called Turkish Super Cup.
  3. Turkish Super Cup
    • Played with the top 4 teams
    • In most recent events; Galatasaray lost second place in the final to their feircest rivals Fenerbahce on January 10th, 2026

Whereabouts

Galatasaray is located on the EUROPEAN side of Istanbul. Make sure to never confuse the sides, for the asian side of Istanbul rests Galatasaray's ferciest rival--Fenerbahce.

Galatasaray's stadiums official name is Ali Sami Yen Spor Kompleksi, however, it is usually referred to as RAMS park because of the stadiums sponsor, Nike.

The Bosphorus Srait to the right of the stadium marks the start of the asian side of Istanbul
